Queue use FIFO.

It is always made with front, back variables. Both front and back assigned -1 that indicates its empty. 

It has 3 functions:
Push    (Enqueue)
Pop	(Dequeue)
Peek 	(Show Value that will be returned if pop is used (front value))

Both Push and Pop have 2 conditions 1 for empty and full.
and other for inserting and deleting.
