Sorting : These Techniques are used to sort arrays in ascending or descending form.

Bubble Sort:
Bubble while poring water in glass, bubbles come at top. (At the end of array).Swap adjacent elements again and again if they are in the wrong order.
Passes : n-1 (Outer Loop)

Selection Sort:
Array divided into sorted and unsorted part. Select min/max element from unsorted part and place it in the end of sorted array.
Passes : n-1

Selection Sort:
Like Playing cards, it select the element and place at correct position. It compare with all element at left side.
Passes : n-1
